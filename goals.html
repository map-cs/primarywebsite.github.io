Project focus

MAP-CS explores how computer science can be designed and sustained as a mission-aligned program within liberal arts institutions, with attention to curriculum, pathways, and institutional change.

Structured curriculum review process

The MAP-CS project has developed a literature-informed backward design process to help departments build distinctive, mission-aligned curricula. This process is supported by a step-by-step workbook, a spreadsheet tool for curriculum mapping, and related resources.

On-site working groups

Facilitators from the MAP-CS group can travel to campus to work individually with faculty in a department. These sessions guide teams through the design process and support departmental decision making.

Curricular design retreatsGuidance through review and revisionsFacilitating decision making and change

Virtual cohort working groups

Departments can also participate virtually as part of a cohort of schools. Facilitators lead regular online sessions and remain available for individual meetings with participating institutions.
